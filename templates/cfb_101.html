CFB Betting 101

New to college football betting, or just want the numbers to make sense? Here’s the whole thing in plain English — the three bet types, the words you’ll see, what each of our tools does, and the honest truths most people learn the hard way.
The 3 ways to bet a game

The Spread (ATS)

The favorite gives points; the underdog gets them. You’re betting whether a team wins by more (or loses by less) than that number. “ATS” = against the spread.

Georgia −14 means Georgia must win by 15+ to “cover.” Win by 10? They won the game but lost ATS.

The Total (Over/Under)

The book posts a number for the combined points both teams score. You bet whether the real total lands over or under it.

Total 52.5. Final is 31–24 = 55 → the over cashes. 20–17 = 37 → the under.

The Moneyline

The simplest bet: just pick who wins, any margin. Favorites pay less than you risk (−250); underdogs pay more (+200).

−250 favorite: risk $250 to win $100. +200 dog: risk $100 to win $200.
Words you’ll see
Cover
To beat the spread. A favorite covers by winning by more than the number; a dog covers by keeping it closer (or winning outright).
Push
A tie against the number (win by exactly the spread, or total lands on the number). Your stake is refunded.
The number / line
The spread or total the book is offering. It moves as money comes in — getting a better number is a real edge.
Juice / vig (−110)
The book’s cut. Most spreads and totals cost −110: risk $110 to win $100. That’s why you need to hit ~52.4% just to break even.
Implied probability
What the odds say the chance is. +250 implies ~29%; −250 implies ~71%. Long odds (a +800 anytime-TD) imply a low chance — and cost the most when they miss.
ATS record
A team’s wins–losses against the spread, not their real record. A 12–1 team can be a losing ATS bet if it never covered the big numbers.
Pace
How fast a team plays (plays per game). More plays = more scoring chances = totals lean over. Grind-it-out teams lean under.

5 honest truths (the ones that save money)
1The market is smart. Most trends land near 50% because the books already priced them in. Our tools help you find the spots and context — they are not guaranteed winners.
2Big favorites are a coin flip ATS. Winning by a lot is hard; a 40-point favorite covers under half the time. Don’t assume the good team covers.
3Longshot overs lose. The +600/+900 anytime-TD tickets feel fun but bleed money over time — a miss costs the most at long odds. We flag them for you.
4Shop for the number. Half a point matters. The same bet at −3 vs −3.5 is a different bet — take the better side.
5Context isn’t a lock. A team going 28–12 in a spot is a strong tell, not a promise. Bet what you can afford to lose, and treat every number as odds, not a certainty.
