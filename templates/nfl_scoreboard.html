How to read it honestly: the ROI is a flat 1-unit bet on every priced prop at its real closing price — a measured record, not a projection. A high UNDER hit rate usually doesn’t mean free money: the under is normally the juiced side (priced around −130/−150 because it hits more), so ROI — not hit rate — is what tells you if a side actually paid. This is a tiny sample right now; it needs several weeks before it means anything, and out-of-sample is the only version that counts.
