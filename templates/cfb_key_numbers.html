How to read it honestly: this is point-buying context, not a predictive edge — the market knows about key numbers too. CFB margins spike at 3 and 7 (the field goal and the touchdown), then 10/14/17/21, but the curve is flatter than the NFL — college has more blowouts, so a half-point matters, just less than in the pros. A spread on a key is where buying or selling that half-point is worth the most (and where a push is live); a line that has moved across a key tells you the market already paid for it.
