Defense vs Channel

You don’t bet positions — you bet production channels (rush yds, rec yds, receptions). An RB’s rushing and receiving are different defenses to beat; a WR can carry it (gadget); a QB can run. This shows what players in a channel have actually done against this defense — the game log, the efficiency allowed, the rank, and a thin-sample flag.
