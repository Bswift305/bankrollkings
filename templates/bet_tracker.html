How this is scored — honestly: ROI is flat-stake: net profit divided by total dollars risked (pushes excluded from risk). CLV is the gap between the implied probability of the price you got and the price the market closed at — positive means you locked a better number than the close. CLV is the most reliable long-run signal there is, but it needs a real sample; a handful of bets tells you little. Win rate and a hot week don’t prove an edge — beating the close consistently does. 21+
