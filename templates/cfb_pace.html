How to read it honestly: pace is context for a total, not a projection or an edge — the market prices tempo, and this read is not opponent-adjusted (the matchup card’s totals read does the adjusted number). The league averages about {{ pc_avg_env }} combined points a game. “Plays/gm” is each offense’s snap volume (higher = faster); “their games avg” is how many combined points this team’s games have actually produced. When two fast, high-scoring teams meet into a total that hasn’t caught up, that’s an over-leaning environment — but most totals already bake the pace in, which is why few games lean.
