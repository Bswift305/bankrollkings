Start Here

You don’t need to learn 30 tools. The whole platform is one loop — find an idea, pressure-test it, build the ticket, then measure how you actually did. Here’s the five-minute version. Every tool in the sidebar is grouped under these same five steps.
The one idea
We help you make better decisions — not predict winners. Markets are mostly efficient, so the job isn’t finding magic locks, it’s building a repeatable, defensible process. You’ll judge yourself by the quality of the decision, not last week’s scoreboard. A good bet can lose; a bad bet can win.
1
Discoverfind a play
Scan the whole board for ideas — options, not one pick. Browse every leg on The Menu, let Today’s Cards auto-build a starting point, or work a sport from its Hub.
The Menu → Today’s Cards NFL Hub
2
Evaluatepressure-test it
Before you bet it, look for the reasons it’s wrong. Is the number a real floor or a trap (Prop Floor)? Is your ticket all-overs or over-concentrated (Ticket Check)? What context are you missing (Game Context)?
Ticket Check → Prop Floor Game Context
This step is what makes us different from a picks site. A picks site says “here’s the play.” We say “here’s the play — now let’s look for why it’s wrong.”
3
Buildthe ticket
Assemble your parlay. Tap legs on The Menu and the price updates as you go, or take a ready-made Daily Card and swap legs by eye. The ticket — not the board — is the destination.
Build on The Menu → Today’s Cards
4
Tracklog what you bet
Write down what you actually bet — the price you got and, when you can, the closing line. That one habit is what turns a hunch streak into a measurable record.
Open Bet Tracker →
5
Learnmeasure reality
Did you beat the closing line (CLV)? That’s the honest read on whether your process is sound — more than any single week’s win or loss. Your Tracker shows your CLV and ROI; Track Record shows how the boards themselves have graded.
Your CLV & ROI → Track Record
CLV needs a real sample — a hot handful of bets proves nothing. Beating the close consistently is the signal.

Pick the surface you want to open to every time — the whole board, the finished cards, heat & trends, matchups, or the full dashboard. You can change it whenever. And every tool stays in the sidebar, grouped under these same five steps.
