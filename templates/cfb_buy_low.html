Buy Low / Sell High · teams

College player props are thin, so this runs at the team level. The sticky signal is quality — our opponent-adjusted rating (SRS). The noisy one is how a team has played against that rating lately. Buy Low = a good team playing below its level recently (the number may come softer). Sell High = a weak team playing above it (the number may be inflated). A regression read, not a “due” call.
A quality-vs-recent-results lens, not a prediction — a team whose level and recent play have diverged, worth a look at the number, not a call that they cover this week.

How to use this — honestly: a Research Engine idea board, not a prediction. “Quality” is our opponent-adjusted SRS (points vs an average team, schedule-controlled); the recent read is each game’s margin vs the SRS-expected margin, so a tough recent slate isn’t mistaken for a slump. A team playing below its rating often gets a softer number than its real level — and a weak team on a hot run often gets an inflated one — but the market prices quality too, and early-season samples are small.
