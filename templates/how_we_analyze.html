Our Methodology

Five ways to analyze a bet. We use all five.

Most sites pick one angle and oversell it. A betting decision is only honest when you’ve run it through every discipline that has real evidence behind it — and said out loud how much each one actually proves. Here’s the framework the whole platform is built on, and exactly where each piece lives.
01

Fundamental & Situational

Read the game itself

Matchups, injuries, quarterback and backup tier, weather, rest, scheme, motivation — the why behind a number. Most “situational angles” (revenge, letdown, lookahead) are myths the market already priced, so we test them and refuse to sell the ones that don’t hold. The ones that do — QB value, wind on totals — we surface.

02

Quantitative & Model-Based

Build our own number

A team-strength and prop model that owes nothing to the betting line — power ratings from game results only, and a prop score fit on real graded outcomes. Market-independent on purpose: it’s the one number you can honestly hold against the market instead of just echoing it.

03

Market & Line Value

Respect the sharpest opponent in the room

The closing line is the best single estimate of a game that exists — so we track where the number moves, shop for the best price, and measure closing-line value. When our model and the market disagree, that gap is the only honest definition of an edge. We never claim to see “sharp money” we can’t actually observe.

04

Statistical, Trends & Regression

Base rates, and luck vs. skill

What’s sticky and what isn’t. Records built on turnover margin, one-score games and red-zone luck regress; streaks and “hot hands” are usually already in the price. We show the trend — who’s covering, who’s due to fall — but label it as context, and we live by one rule: out-of-sample or it doesn’t count.

05

Risk & Bankroll Management

The discipline that keeps you in the game

This one doesn’t pick winners — it makes an edge survive variance. Flat unit sizing, expected value over hit rate, and the guardrails that stop the bets that quietly bleed you: long-odds overs, all-over parlays, single-book prices. The best handicap in the world loses if the staking blows up the roll on a normal cold streak.

How we weight them

They aren’t equal. We anchor on the market (03) because it’s the sharpest estimate available, and hold everything to the discipline of (04) — nothing counts until it survives out-of-sample. Our own model (02) is a market-independent second opinion, fundamentals (01) are the context that explains the number, and risk management (05) is baked into every guardrail so a good read isn’t undone by a bad stake.

That’s why you’ll never see a single magic “1–100” score here. We label each item by its source and current governance status — a documented backtest result, a model estimate, or a situational note. No item is described as a validated edge unless a governance record authorizes that claim.

The honesty layer on top of all five

  • Source + governance status on every item — a documented backtest, a model estimate, or a situational note; nothing is called a validated edge without a governance record.
  • “Assumes independence” on every combined-probability number — parlays compound the vig, and we say so.
  • Availability states (Verified / Partial / Pending / Stale) so missing context never reads as neutral.
  • No “sharp,” “steam,” or “lock” language. We don’t claim to see money we can’t.
  • Out-of-sample or it doesn’t count. The rules that looked unbeatable in-sample and reversed taught us this the expensive way.
