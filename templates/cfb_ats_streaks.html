How to read it honestly: a streak is a trend the market already prices — our graded work shows streaks live in the number, so this is “who’s been beating the spread (or total) lately,” a starting point and your eye test, not a proven edge. The result string is the last few games (newest last); “by” is the average margin vs the closing consensus number. A team crushing the number every week is as likely to have been underrated as to be due for a market correction — the line moves to catch up.
