How to read it honestly: this is regression to the mean, not a pick. Point differential predicts a team’s rest-of-season record better than its current W–L — a team winning more games than its scoring margin supports (the − number here) has been getting the bounces, and a team with a fat differential and a poor record (the + number) has been unlucky. One-score games are close to a coin flip over time, so a gaudy close-game record rarely holds. Turnover margin swings hard week to week. None of this says who wins Sunday — it says which records are built on sand, so you don’t overpay a lucky team or fade an unlucky one on name alone.
