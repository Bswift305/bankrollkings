Why this, not a streak board: we proved streaks get priced fast — but opportunity is sticky and it’s the real reason a player keeps hitting. A 45%-target-share receiver or a back who owns his backfield has a high floor whether or not he’s “hot.” That’s the honest version of following the hot hand. Tap any player to see how often he actually clears his lines. Opportunity score is transparent: target/air-yards share for pass-catchers, carry share + pass role for backs, and yards per touch (the rate behind the per-game number). The ↻ script tag flags the minority of players whose share actually swings with game-state (leading vs trailing) — an early-down back fades if his team trails, a chase-script receiver eats when behind — so for them raw season share can mislead; for everyone else it’s clean. The Locked / Volatile tag is Role Stability — how much the share moves week to week. A floor isn’t just high opportunity, it’s high opportunity with a stable role: two backs can both average 60% of the carries, but one ranges 58–62% (a floor) and the other 35–85% on script (no floor). Early-season samples are small — the number firms up weekly. 21+
