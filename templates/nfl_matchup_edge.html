How to read it honestly: this is the own-data version of a “vs man coverage” note. We don’t license proprietary man/zone charting, so instead of claiming a coverage split we can’t source, we combine three things we can stand behind: usage (how many targets and air yards a receiver commands), separation (NGS — how much room he actually gets open, league average ~3.0 yds), and the opponent’s pass-defense rank this week (1 = toughest, 32 = softest). A high-usage receiver who creates separation into a soft pass D is the plus spot; the edge number weighs all three. It’s context for a receiving prop, not a projection — early-season separation is a small sample and firms up weekly.{% if updated %} Updated {{ updated }}.{% endif %}
